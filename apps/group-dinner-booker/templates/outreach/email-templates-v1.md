# Large-Party Outreach Email Templates (v1)

Use placeholders in `{{double_braces}}`.

---

## 1) Formal (default)

**Template ID:** `email_formal_v1`

**Subject options:**
- Large Party Inquiry — {{event_date}} ({{party_size}} guests)
- Group Dining Request: {{party_size}} Guests on {{event_date}}

**Body:**

Hello {{restaurant_name}} Team,

I’m reaching out to ask about availability for a group dinner at {{restaurant_name}}.

- Date: {{event_date}}
- Preferred time: {{time_window}}
- Party size: {{party_size}} (final range: {{headcount_range}})
- Budget target: {{budget_note}}
- Dietary/accessibility notes: {{dietary_accessibility_notes}}

If available, could you share your group dining options and terms (minimum spend, deposit, menu format, and cancellation policy)?

We can also consider {{fallback_time_option_1}} or {{fallback_time_option_2}} if that helps.

Thank you,
{{sender_name}}
{{sender_contact}}

---

## 2) Concise

**Template ID:** `email_concise_v1`

**Subject:** Quick Group Dining Availability Check — {{event_date}}

**Body:**

Hi {{restaurant_name}} team — do you have availability for {{party_size}} guests on {{event_date}} around {{time_window}}?

Key details:
- Headcount range: {{headcount_range}}
- Budget: {{budget_note}}
- Notes: {{dietary_accessibility_notes}}

If yes, please share terms (minimum spend/deposit/menu style).

Best,
{{sender_name}}
{{sender_contact}}

## 3) Warm / Celebratory

We’re looking for:
- {{party_size}} guests on {{event_date}}

{{sender_name}}
{{sender_contact}}

## Follow-Up Snippets

### Follow-Up #1 (Day 2)
Subject: Re: Group Dining Request — {{event_date}}

Hi {{restaurant_name}} Team,

Just checking in on the request below for {{event_date}} ({{party_size}} guests). Happy to adjust timing if needed.

Would you be able to share availability and terms?

Thank you,
{{sender_name}}

### Follow-Up #2 (Day 5)
Subject: Quick yes/no on {{event_date}} group request?

Hi {{restaurant_name}} Team,

Friendly follow-up in case this was missed — if unavailable, a quick no is very helpful so we can finalize plans.

Thanks,
{{sender_name}}

Hi {{restaurant_name}} Team,

Final check on this inquiry. If we don’t hear back, we’ll release this request and proceed with other options.

Appreciate your time,
{{sender_name}}
