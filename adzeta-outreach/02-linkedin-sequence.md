# AdZeta — LinkedIn Connection Sequence (2-Touch)

## Overview
LinkedIn is the credibility layer. Use it to soften email touches and create multi-channel presence.

---

## Touch 1: Connection Request (Value-First Note)
**Day 2 | Goal: Connect with clear relevance signal**

**Connection Note (300 chars max):**

```
Hi {first_name}, saw {company} just {recent_news} — exciting growth phase. I help RevOps teams at Series A-C SaaS companies unify their GTM data. Would love to connect and share relevant insights as you scale.
```

**Alternative Notes (Rotate by Signal):**

**Funding Signal:**
```
Hi {first_name}, congrats on the {funding_round} — saw {company} is hiring aggressively in sales. Connecting because I help RevOps teams keep pipeline visibility as they scale. Worth being connected?
```

**Hiring Signal (RevOps):**
```
Hi {first_name}, saw {company} is building out RevOps — smart move at your stage. I help teams unify GTM data and reduce forecast chaos. Would love to connect and share what we're seeing across Series A-C SaaS.
```

**Tech Stack Gap Signal:**
```
Hi {first_name}, noticed {company} is using {tool_1} + {tool_2} — common stack. I help RevOps teams unify GTM data across fragmented tools. Worth connecting to share relevant playbook?
```

**Generic/Icebreaker:**
```
Hi {first_name}, I work with RevOps leaders at SaaS companies like {company} to solve GTM visibility challenges. Would love to connect and be a resource as you scale.
```

## Touch 2: Follow-Up Message (Soft Pitch)
**Day 7 | Goal: Brief value exchange + soft meeting ask**

**Prerequisites:**
- Prospect accepted connection (Day 2-6)
- No email reply received (if they replied, skip this)

**Message:**

```
Thanks for connecting, {first_name}.

Quick context on why I reached out: We're seeing a pattern with Series A-C SaaS teams like {company}'s — rapid growth creates GTM data fragmentation. Marketing and Sales end up working from different truths, and RevOps spends more time reconciling than optimizing.

We built AdZeta to fix this — a GTM Command Center that unifies pipeline visibility and cuts forecast prep from hours to minutes.

Given {company}'s recent {recent_news}, wondered if this is on your radar?

Happy to share what we're seeing across {employee_range}-person SaaS teams if useful — no pitch, just intel.
```

**Soft CTA Variants (test by persona):**

**For RevOps/VPs:**
```
Curious — what's your biggest pain point in pipeline visibility right now? Happy to share how similar teams solved it.
```

**For CMOs:**
```
Question: Are you getting real-time visibility into CAC payback by channel? It's the #1 ask we're hearing from marketing leaders.
```

**For VPs Sales:**
```
Quick question: How long does pipeline review prep take your team? We're helping teams cut it from 6 hours to 12 minutes.
```

## LinkedIn Engagement Rules

### Profile Prerequisites
- [ ] Sender profile optimized (headline, banner, featured section)
- [ ] About section mentions GTM Command Center / RevOps focus
- [ ] Featured post: case study or GTM insight (pinned)
- [ ] Recent activity: 2-3 posts in past 30 days

### Connection Limits
- Max 20-30 connection requests/day per sender
- Space requests throughout day (don't burst)
- Withdraw pending requests > 3 weeks old

### Response Handling
| Prospect Action | Next Step |
|-----------------|-----------|
| Accepts + no message | Send Touch 2 (Day 7) |
| Accepts + positive message | Move to email, accelerate to demo |
| Accepts + negative message | Respectful close, tag for nurture |
| Doesn't accept (7 days) | Continue email sequence, LinkedIn deprioritized |

### Messaging Best Practices
- Keep connection notes under 300 characters (hard limit)
- Follow-up messages under 150 words
- One CTA max per message
- No external links in LinkedIn messages (penalized)
- Respond to replies within 4 hours if possible
