# AdZeta GTM Operating Cadence (v1)

## Purpose
Create a consistent operating rhythm that improves forecast accuracy, pipeline quality, deal velocity, and cross-functional execution.

---

## Daily Rhythm (Mon–Fri)

### 1) Daily Revenue Pulse (15 min)
- **When:** Start of business day
- **Owner:** CRO (Jim)
- **Attendees:** Sales leadership, RevOps (optional: CS lead)
- **Inputs:** New pipeline, stage movements, slips, closed-won/lost, at-risk deals
- **Outputs:**
  - Top 3 priorities for the day
  - Named owners for urgent deal actions
  - Escalations for blockers (pricing/legal/product)

### 2) End-of-Day Deal Delta (async, 5–10 min)
- **When:** End of day
- **Owner:** AEs + managers
- **Format:** Short update in shared channel/doc
- **Outputs:**
  - What changed today
  - What is blocked
  - Next action + due date

---

## Weekly Rhythm

### Monday: Forecast Call (45–60 min)
- **Goal:** Commit/Best/Upside quality + risk-adjusted forecast confidence
- **Outputs:**
  - Weekly forecast view by segment/owner
  - Top risks and mitigation plan
  - Executive escalation list

### Tuesday: Pipeline Inspection (60 min)
- **Goal:** Diagnose pipeline health and conversion quality
- **Core metrics:**
  - Coverage ratio
  - Stage conversion rates
  - Aging by stage
  - Pipeline source mix
  - New pipeline created vs target
- **Outputs:**
  - 3 pipeline interventions for the week
  - Owner and deadline for each intervention

### Wednesday: Deal Strategy Review (60 min)
- **Goal:** Advance top strategic deals and unblock stuck deals
- **Scope:** Top 10–20 active opportunities
- **Outputs:**
  - Mutual action plans for top deals
  - Clear close plan and risk flags
  - Cross-functional asks (product, legal, finance)

### Thursday: GTM Systems + Enablement (45 min)
- **Goal:** Improve process, messaging, and execution quality
- **Topics:** Objection trends, win/loss signals, stage hygiene, tooling/process fixes
- **Outputs:**
  - 1 process fix
  - 1 enablement action
  - 1 instrumentation/data-quality fix

### Friday: Executive GTM Brief (30 min prep + distribution)
- **Owner:** CRO + Al
- **Audience:** Exec stakeholders
- **Format:** One-page summary
- **Sections:**
  - Forecast status (vs plan)
  - Pipeline health snapshot
  - Major wins/losses and learnings
  - Risks + mitigation
  - Next week’s top priorities

---

## Monthly Rhythm

### 1) Monthly GTM Performance Review (90 min)
- **Goal:** Evaluate performance vs targets and reset priorities
- **Outputs:**
  - What worked / what didn’t
  - Top 3 strategic adjustments
  - Updated priorities for next month

### 2) Win/Loss + Message Review (60 min)
- **Goal:** Tighten positioning, objection handling, and qualification
- **Outputs:**
  - Pattern summary
  - Messaging updates
  - Sales play updates

### 3) Territory/Segment Review (60 min)
- **Goal:** Allocate effort where return is highest
- **Outputs:**
  - Segment-level performance diagnosis
  - Resource shifts and coverage plan

---

## KPI Spine (tracked weekly, reviewed monthly)
- Pipeline coverage
- New pipeline creation vs target
- Stage conversion rates
- Average sales cycle by segment
- Forecast accuracy
- Win rate
- Deal slippage rate
- Average deal size

---

## Operating Rules
1. Every meeting ends with owners + deadlines.
2. No metric without an action owner.
3. Exceptions/escalations are explicit, not implied.
4. Keep artifacts lightweight: one source of truth per rhythm.
5. Weekly cadence feeds monthly strategic decisions.
