# AdZeta — Operator Briefing Templates (Daily + Weekly)

_Last updated: 2026-02-22_

## How to use
- **Daily briefing** is optimized for **time-to-next-human-action**.
- **Weekly briefing** is optimized for **pilot conversion** and strategic iteration.
- Every section should include links/IDs to evidence (threads, ledger entries, account ids).
