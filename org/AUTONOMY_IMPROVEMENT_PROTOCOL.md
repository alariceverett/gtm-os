# AUTONOMY_IMPROVEMENT_PROTOCOL.md

## Objective
Maximize autonomous execution quality by tying all work to business outcomes and enforcing a deterministic execution chain.

## Non-negotiable execution chain (every cycle)
1) Decision recorded (objective + expected outcome)
2) Immediate delegation/spawn
3) Apply to live product
4) Verify with evidence (URL/status/flow proof)
5) Operator update (material only)
6) Upstream Forge update for systemic gaps

If any step is missed => `AUTONOMY_GAP` incident.

## Outcome gates (must map to at least one)
- Qualified accounts/week
- Positive replies + meetings booked
- Pilot candidate conversion
- Time-to-next-human-action

## Quality gates (must pass before “done”)
- Purpose clarity in <30 seconds
- Core flow <=5 clicks
- Enterprise UI consistency baseline
- Critical routes healthy (HTTP 200)

## Prioritization rule
If a task does not improve a measured business outcome or unblock a quality/reliability gate, deprioritize.

## Reporting cadence
- Material update only, with proof:
  - exact URL(s)
  - what changed visibly
  - outcome impact
  - remaining blockers + next action
