## Research Methodology

### Selection Criteria
- **Funding Stage:** Series A-C (or late-stage with similar profile)
- **Employee Count:** 50-500 (mid-market sweet spot)
- **Business Model:** B2B SaaS with sales-led motion
- **Growth Signals:** Recent funding (2021-2024), hiring expansion, product launches
- **Pain Point Indicators:** GTM complexity, multiple tool stacks, RevOps relevance

### Data Sources
- Crunchbase funding data
- LinkedIn company pages and employee growth
- Company press releases and blogs
- Job posting analysis (RevOps, sales ops hiring)
- Industry reports and SaaS funding databases

### Scoring Framework (1-5 Scale)

**Score 5 (High Priority)**
- Recent major funding (2023-2024)
- Explicit RevOps leadership / hiring
- Direct GTM complexity signals
- Clear tool fragmentation issues

**Score 4 (Qualified Priority)**
- Strong funding signal (2021-2023)
- Sales/CMO leadership identifiable
- Business model suggests GTM complexity
- Growing team with operational challenges

**Score 3 (Lower Priority)**
- Stable funding but older (2020-2021)
- Acquired or public company status
- Smaller team size (<100)
- Niche use case with limited scale
