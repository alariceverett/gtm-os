# Q1/Q2 GTM Planning Calendar
## 2026 Planning Guide for Revenue Operations Leaders

**Seasonal campaigns, budget cycles, and industry events to mark on your calendar**

---

## Q1: The Foundation Quarter

### January — Planning & Alignment Month

**Key Activities:**
- [ ] Finalize FY26 GTM strategy and budgeting
- [ ] Set Q1 OKRs across Marketing, Sales, CS
- [ ] Audit and refresh ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
- [ ] Review and renew annual software contracts
- [ ] Launch new year messaging campaigns

**Budget Cycle:** ⚠️ **CRITICAL** — Most enterprise budget approvals for new vendors happen Jan-Feb

**Campaign Themes:**
- "New Year, New Stack" — tech refresh messaging
- "Q1 Planning" — productivity and goal-setting content
- Fiscal year planning for companies with calendar year-end

---

### February — Execution & Optimization Month

**Key Activities:**
- [ ] Execute ABM campaigns targeting FY26 budget holders
- [ ] Launch Q1 pipeline generation initiatives
- [ ] Optimize Q4 learnings and apply to new campaigns
- [ ] Conduct mid-Q1 pipeline review
- [ ] Begin planning for Sales Kick Off (SKO)

**Budget Cycle:** 💰 **SPENDING OPENS** — Approved budgets begin getting deployed

**Campaign Themes:**
- "Love Your Data" — data hygiene and integrity campaigns
- Valentine's Day themed prospecting (lighthearted)
- Fiscal planning content for growing companies

**Trade Shows & Events:**
| Event | Date | Location | Focus |
|-------|------|----------|-------|
| SaaStr Annual | Feb 11-13 | SF Bay Area | B2B SaaS scaling |
| RevOps Summit | Feb 18-20 | Austin, TX | Revenue Operations |
| G2 Reach | Feb 25-27 | Chicago, IL | Product reviews & growth |

---

### March — Quarter-End Push & SKO Prep

**Key Activities:**
- [ ] Q1 close and pipeline cleanup
- [ ] Sales Kick Off (SKO) execution
- [ ] Q2 campaign and content planning
- [ ] Mid-year budget check-ins with key prospects
- [ ] Renew Q2-Q3 pipeline for calendar-year companies

**Budget Cycle:** ⏰ **Q1 SPEND DEADLINE** — Use-it-or-lose-it budgets expire

**Campaign Themes:**
- "Spring Cleaning" — data audit and optimization
- SKO content and enablement themes
- "March Madness" — competitive bracket campaigns (trend-jacking)

---

## Q2: The Acceleration Quarter

### April — SKO Aftermath & Pipeline Build

**Key Activities:**
- [ ] Activate SKO energy into pipeline generation
- [ ] Launch Q2 campaigns across segments
- [ ] Review Q1 performance and optimize
- [ ] Begin mid-year forecast reviews
- [ ] Plan for summer slow-down mitigation

**Budget Cycle:** 📊 **Q2 BUDGETS RELEASED** — New fiscal year budgets for many companies

**Campaign Themes:**
- "Tax Season Relief" — ROI and efficiency content
- Spring renewal/re-engagement campaigns
- Earth Day themes (sustainability in operations)

---

### May — Peak Pipeline Generation

**Key Activities:**
- [ ] Execute major Q2 pipeline plays
- [ ] Mid-year customer success reviews
- [ ] Optimize funnel conversion rates
- [ ] Plan summer content for decision-makers
- [ ] Begin Q3 territory and quota planning

**Budget Cycle:** 💰 **MID-YEAR SPENDING WAVE** — Procurement accelerates before summer

**Campaign Themes:**
- "May the Fourth" — Star Wars themed (lighthearted engagement)
- Mother's Day analogies (nurturing leads, etc.)
- Memorial Day — productivity and time-saving themes

---

**Key Activities:**

**Budget Cycle:** ⏰ **H1 CLOSEOUT** — Critical for fiscal year companies

**Campaign Themes:**
- "Half-Time Report" — mid-year performance content
- "Summer Reading" — long-form educational content
- "Hit Your H1 Goals" — urgency-driven campaigns

## Pro Tips for Revenue Leaders

1. **Front-load Q1:** Decision-makers are freshest and most receptive in January
2. **Beat the summer slow-down:** Close critical deals by mid-June
3. **Plan SKO content:** Use January to build SKO enablement materials
4. **Budget for conferences:** Book Q1-Q2 events by December for early-bird pricing
5. **Align content calendars:** Map blog, social, and email to budget cycles
