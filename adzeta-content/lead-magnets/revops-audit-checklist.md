# RevOps Audit Checklist
## Score Your GTM Maturity in 10 Minutes

**A comprehensive health check for Revenue Operations leaders**

---

## Introduction

This checklist evaluates your Go-to-Market (GTM) operations across critical dimensions. Score each item honestly to identify gaps and prioritize improvements.

**Scoring Guide:**
- ✅ **Yes (2 points)** — Fully implemented and optimized
- 🔄 **Partial (1 point)** — Implemented but needs improvement
- ❌ **No (0 points)** — Not implemented

---

## The 10-Point GTM Health Checklist

### 1. Data Architecture & Integration
**Are your core systems (CRM, marketing automation, sales engagement, billing) fully integrated with clean data flows?**

- [ ] Real-time sync between CRM and marketing automation
- [ ] Single source of truth for customer data
- [ ] Automated data validation and hygiene processes
- [ ] Clear data ownership and governance framework

**Why it matters:** Broken data flows create blind spots, duplicate efforts, and poor customer experiences.

---

### 2. Lead-to-Revenue Process
**Do you have a documented, measured lead-to-revenue process with clear stage definitions?**

- [ ] Documented funnel stages (MQL → SQL → Opportunity → Customer)
- [ ] Clear exit criteria for each stage
- [ ] Service Level Agreements (SLAs) between marketing and sales
- [ ] Automated lead routing and scoring

**Why it matters:** Undefined processes lead to leads falling through cracks and misaligned teams.

---

### 3. Revenue Attribution
**Can you accurately attribute revenue to specific marketing channels, campaigns, and touchpoints?**

- [ ] Multi-touch attribution model implemented
- [ ] UTM parameter hygiene and governance
- [ ] Campaign influence reporting for pipeline
- [ ] CAC and LTV tracking by acquisition source

**Why it matters:** Without attribution, you're flying blind on where to invest your marketing budget.

---

### 4. Sales Velocity & Pipeline Management
**Do you track and optimize sales velocity metrics across pipeline stages?**

- [ ] Average deal age monitored by stage
- [ ] Win/loss rate trends analyzed regularly
- [ ] Pipeline coverage ratio >3x target
- [ ] Automated pipeline hygiene alerts

**Why it matters:** Slow velocity kills momentum and forecasting accuracy.

---

### 5. Forecasting Accuracy
**Is your revenue forecasting process data-driven with >80% accuracy?**

- [ ] Weighted pipeline forecasting methodology
- [ ] Historical close rate analysis by segment
- [ ] Committed vs. best case categories defined
- [ ] Weekly forecast reviews with leadership

**Why it matters:** Missed forecasts erode board confidence and lead to reactive decision-making.

---

### 6. Tech Stack Optimization
**Is your GTM tech stack evaluated quarterly for ROI and redundancy?**

- [ ] Tech stack inventory with ownership documented
- [ ] Utilization tracking across all tools
- [ ] Quarterly ROI analysis on each platform
- [ ] Integration health monitoring

**Why it matters:** Tool sprawl drains budget and creates data silos.

---

### 7. Automation & Efficiency
**What percentage of repetitive GTM tasks are automated?**

- [ ] Lead enrichment and qualification automated
- [ ] Reporting and dashboards auto-delivered
- [ ] Sales follow-up sequences automated
- [ ] Customer onboarding workflows streamlined

**Why it matters:** Manual processes don't scale and burn out your best people.

---

### 8. Cross-Functional Alignment
**Do RevOps, Sales, Marketing, and Customer Success operate from shared metrics?**

- [ ] Unified North Star metric defined
- [ ] Cross-functional weekly standups
- [ ] Shared dashboards visible to all GTM teams
- [ ] Joint OKRs across departments

**Why it matters:** Siloed metrics create conflicting priorities and internal friction.

---

### 9. Customer Journey Visibility
**Do you have end-to-end visibility across the entire customer lifecycle?**

- [ ] Journey mapping from first touch to renewal
- [ ] Health score monitoring post-sale
- [ ] Expansion revenue tracking
- [ ] Churn signal detection and alerts

**Why it matters:** You can't optimize what you can't measure across the full lifecycle.

---

### 10. Scalable Operating Cadence
**Do you have a repeatable planning and review rhythm that scales?**

- [ ] Monthly GTM business reviews
- [ ] Quarterly strategy planning sessions
- [ ] Annual OKR and budget alignment
- [ ] Playbook documentation and version control

**Why it matters:** Growth creates complexity; systems must scale with you.
