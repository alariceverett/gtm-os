🎯 Marketing confidence: Finally knew which campaigns actually drove revenue

Reps stopped fighting over leads. The system was fair, transparent, and fast.

## Post 3: "The RevOps Tech Stack Audit"
*Theme: Education | Goal: Establish authority and drive tool-related discussions*

---

**The RevOps Tech Stack Audit: 15 questions to ask before your next renewal**

Your CRM costs $X/month. Your marketing automation costs $Y/month. Your sales engagement platform costs $Z/month.

But what's the **total cost of ownership?**

Use this audit before signing your next contract:

**🔍 UTILIZATION (The low-hanging fruit)**
- What % of licenses are actually being used?
- How many users haven't logged in in 90+ days?
- Are you paying for features nobody uses?

**🔍 INTEGRATION HEALTH**
- Does it sync bidirectionally with your CRM?
- How often do sync errors occur?
- What's the latency on data updates?

**🔍 DATA HYGIENE**
- Can you trust the data in this system?
- How much duplicate data does it create?
- What's the cleanup effort required?

**🔍 ROI MEASUREMENT**
- Can you attribute revenue to this tool?
- What's your cost per won deal that touched this tool?
- Would you buy it again today at this price?

**🔍 OVERLAP ANALYSIS**
- What does this tool do that 3 other tools also do?
- Could you consolidate without losing functionality?
- What's the switching cost if you moved?

---

**My hot take:** Most companies are over-tooling their GTM stack.

It's not about having the "best" tool for each job. It's about having the right tools that actually talk to each other.

Tool sprawl is a tax on your operations team that compounds every quarter.

Audit before you renew. Every time.

## Post 4: "AI vs. Human in GTM"
*Theme: Trend/Opinion | Goal: Thought leadership engagement*

---

**AI won't replace RevOps. RevOps who use AI will replace RevOps who don't.**

I've been testing AI tools across the GTM stack for 18 months. Here's what's working (and what's hype):

**✅ AI is GREAT at:**
- **Data enrichment** (clearbit, zoominfo on AI steroids)
- **Lead scoring** (pattern recognition at scale)
- **Forecasting** (spotting trends humans miss)
- **Report generation** (natural language → dashboards)
- **Email personalization** (at scale, not spam)

**❌ AI is TERRIBLE at:**
- **Strategic decisions** (context matters)
- **Handling exceptions** (edge cases break AI first)
- **Cross-functional negotiation** (alignment takes humans)
- **Change management** (you can't automate buy-in)
- **Judgment calls** (when data is incomplete)

---

**The truth:**

The best RevOps teams I'm seeing aren't replacing themselves with AI. They're using AI to:

→ Automate the work nobody wants to do
→ Surface insights they'd never have time to find
→ Buy back strategic thinking time

**The risk:** Using AI as a crutch instead of a multiplier. Lazy prompting = lazy results.

**My prediction:**

In 3 years, "AI-powered" won't be a differentiator. It'll be table stakes.

But AI *strategy* — knowing what to automate and what to humanize — that's the competitive edge.
